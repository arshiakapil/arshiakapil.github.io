RepeatFocus

A tool for evaluating the recovery of repetitive regions in long-read genome assemblies

RepeatFocus is a reproducible Nextflow-based pipeline designed to assess how well repetitive genomic regions are recovered from low-coverage long-read datasets. The pipeline integrates k-mer analysis, read filtering, repeat-aware assembly, and quantitative assembly evaluation metrics.
